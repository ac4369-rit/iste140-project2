Music Streaming Terms

Below you can find 20 technical terms which frequently appear when referring to music streaming:

Streaming
A technology used for the transfer of data with a view to being captured and processed in a steady system.
Audio Codec
Software in charge of encoding or decoding digital data streams.
Bitrate
Amount of bits transmitted and processed per second, showing the speed at which digital data is capable of being transferred.
Lossy Compression
Data compression method which decreases file size by erasing elements which are not that visible for human beings.
Lossless Audio
Digital sound format which keeps 100% of the original data without losing quality during storage or transmission.
Sampling Rate
Number of samples captured per second from an analogical signal.
Buffering
Keep data in a buffer while is is being processed or transferred.
Caching
Store data in a cache memory.
Content Delivery Network (CDN)
Network of servers that provides web content to users efficiently.
Metadata
Set of data that describes and offers information about other data.
ISRC
12-character international identifier which is used for the permanent identification of a specific sound recording or music video.
Digital Distributor
Person, company or platform in charge of the delivery of information across digital platforms.
Royalties
Amount of money paid to the owner of an asset in exchange for the right to use.
Per-Stream Payout
Amount of money paid every time a song is played on an streaming service.
Collaborative Filtering
Recommendation technique that predicts user's preferences by analysing interests of similar individuals.
Recommendation Algorithm
Decision making system designed to suggest relevant items, content or actions to users.
Audio Fingerprinting
Technology which is implemented for analysing audio content, based on its single characteristics.
Playlist Pitching
Process of submitting non-released music to editorial teams at streaming platforms.
Bit Depth
Number of bits used to represent each sample in digital audio.
Music Production Workflow
Sequence of stages that takes a musical idea from concept to streaming-ready release.
